06Website report

Your website,
on the bench.

Email us your web address and we'll send back a full report on your site, including where it ranks against your five closest competitors.

What's in it

We score the eight things a buyer notices: first impression, clarity of offer, technical detail, mobile, speed, enquiry path, trust signals and SEO basics. Every score comes with what we found and what to do about it. Then your five closest competitors go through the same checks, so you can see where you sit.

Why it has to come from your domain

The report is about your business, so it only goes to someone who can show the site's theirs. If your website is yourfirm.co.uk, send the email from an address ending @yourfirm.co.uk. Any name before the @ is fine. We reply to the address it came from, and nowhere else.

Gmail, Outlook.com, Hotmail, Yahoo and iCloud addresses can't show us that, so we can't send a report to one. Plenty of firms run on a Gmail address and there's nothing wrong with it. If that's you, you can still start a conversation below.
